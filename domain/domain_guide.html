Pokeplan · context for the PokeAPI data

Pokémon Domain Guide

What you need to know about the games to model PokeAPI well. The same move, Pokémon or type matchup can mean different things in different games, and the API only partly says so. Counts are measured on the real data (PokeAPI source, 7 Oct 2026).

Blue boxes say what each fact changes in the star schema.

1A 30-year timeline

The series is split into nine generations. Each adds species, usually a region, and new rules.

  • Versions and version groups. Games come in pairs (Red and Blue) that share almost all data. PokeAPI groups them: 53 versions in 32 version groups. Learnsets hang off the group; encounters off the single version.
  • Generation is release date, not setting. HeartGold/SoulSilver are a gen IV remake of gen II, set in Johto and Kanto. FireRed/LeafGreen, Let's Go and Brilliant Diamond/Shining Pearl are remakes too.
  • The API doesn't say which games are remakes, DLC or spin-offs (Colosseum, XD, Champions), or that Red/Green is Japan-only.

Model: a seed table ref.version_group_info adds release year and game category; bridge_version_group_region lets one game have two regions; version-group order (1 to 32) becomes the timeline for move history.

3Types and the type chart

18 battle types: 15 in gen I, 17 in gens II–V, 18 from gen VI. The API has three more that no Pokémon has as its own typing: Stellar (Tera only), ??? (the move Curse) and Shadow (Colosseum/XD).

Two types multiply. A Water/Flying defender takes 2 × 2 = 4× from Electric and 0× from Ground. Never add the multipliers.

The physical/special split (gen IV). Until Diamond/Pearl a damaging move's class came from its type alone. Normal, Fighting, Flying, Poison, Ground, Rock, Bug, Ghost and Steel moves were physical; Fire, Water, Grass, Electric, Psychic, Ice, Dragon and Dark moves were special. So Fire Punch was special, and Bite was a special move in gens II–III. The API stores only today's class per move.

Model: dim_type.type_role keeps Stellar, ??? and Shadow out of the chart (2,677 rows, 15/17/18 squares); dim_move.effective_damage_class derives the gen I–III class from the move's type in that era.

4Things that change between games

PokeAPI records history in four different shapes. Each needs its own handling.

FieldListsKeyed byRows
past_typesthe whole old typinggeneration, up to and including36
past_statsonly the stats that changedgeneration, up to and including235
past_abilitiesonly changed slots; empty = slot didn't existgeneration, up to and including569
move.past_valuesonly the fields that changedversion group, valid before it198
  • Stats. Gen I had five stats with one Special. Gen VI raised many older Pokémon: Pikachu's Defense went 30 → 40, Sp. Def 40 → 50.
  • Abilities start in gen III, hidden abilities in gen V. Gengar had Levitate until gen VI and Cursed Body from gen VII.
  • Types. 29 Pokémon changed type, mostly the gen VI Fairy retypes (Clefairy was Normal).
  • Moves. 198 recorded changes to power, accuracy, PP, type or effect chance, 83 of them in X/Y.

The data corrects the docs. PokeAPI's docs say past_values.version_group is when the old values applied. Bite lists type: normal, version_group: gold-silver, and Bite was Normal in Red/Blue but Dark from Gold/Silver. So the listed values are the old ones, valid up to that game.

Model: stats, abilities and types are generation-dated facts, clamped so nothing exists before its mechanic or its Pokémon. dim_move is SCD type 2: 1,488 eras for 937 moves, and each learnset row points at the era live in its game.

5Learnsets and TMs

638,321 learnset rows. Methods: machine 372k, level-up 162k, tutor 50k, egg 35k, plus game-specific ones (Colosseum/XD shadow moves, Champions' "train").

  • One move can be learnt at several levels in the same game. Smeargle learns Sketch at levels 1, 11, 21 … 91.
  • Level 0 on a level-up row means "learnt on evolving" (Sun/Moon onwards, 1,092 rows).
  • DLC learnsets are folded into the base game; Legends: Z-A has none yet.

2,372 machine rows: 2,142 TMs, 130 HMs, 100 TRs. TMs were single-use until gen V; HMs went away in gen VII; TRs existed only in gen VIII.

Model: level_learned_at joins the key of fact_pokemon_move (the old key rejected the real data); new fact_machine, one row per TM per game.

6Items and evolution

2,223 items: misc 1,204, key items 391, machines 338, medicine 106, berries 74, Poké Balls 38. Held items arrived in gen II. Items matter in four places: wild held items, evolution, Mega Stones and machines.

540 evolution chains, 676 evolution rules, 18 triggers. Rules combine level, time of day, held item, known move, trade partner, party member, rain, even turning the console upside down (Inkay).

  • Chains branch. Eevee has eight evolutions; Tyrogue compares Attack and Defense.
  • Rules change by game. Magnezone evolved at Mt. Coronet in gen IV and with a Thunder Stone from Sword/Shield.
  • Regional evolutions depend on region and form. Pikachu becomes Alolan Raichu with a Thunder Stone only in Alola.

Model: fact_evolution gains from_pokemon_key, to_pokemon_key and region_code, so Pikachu → Raichu and Pikachu → Alolan Raichu are separate rows.

7Wild encounters

117,127 encounter rows, 66 methods, 30 kinds of condition (time, season, weather, swarm, Poké Radar, GBA cartridge in slot 2, story progress).

Classic games give each area fixed slots with fixed odds, for example 12 grass slots at 20/20/10/10/10/10/5/5/4/4/1/1 %. One Pokémon can fill several slots, so two identical rows are both real.

Coverage stops at Sword/Shield. PokeAPI has no wild-encounter data for Brilliant Diamond/Shining Pearl, Legends: Arceus, Scarlet/Violet or later.

Model: dim_encounter_method.chance_unit says which meaning applies; dim_version_group.has_encounter_data flags the gap.

8Pokédexes

35 Pokédexes: the National Dex plus regional ones, often several per game (Kalos Central, Coastal and Mountain; Sword/Shield's Galar, Isle of Armor and Crown Tundra). Pikachu is #25 nationally and #74 in Paldea. Since Sword/Shield, a game's regional dex decides which Pokémon are in it.

Model: new dim_pokedex, fact_pokedex_entry (8,010 rows) and bridge_version_group_pokedex.

9Lore, briefly

  • Regions are modelled on real places: Kanto, Johto, Hoenn and Sinnoh on Japan; Unova on New York; Kalos on northern France; Alola on Hawaii; Galar on the UK; Paldea on Iberia. Kanto and Johto border each other. Hisui is ancient Sinnoh.
  • Creation myth: Arceus made Dialga (time), Palkia (space) and Giratina, then the lake guardians Uxie, Mesprit and Azelf. Mew is said to carry the DNA of every Pokémon.
  • Professors catalogue each region; Professor Oak's first Pokédex had 151 entries, the National Dex now has 1,025.
  • The League: eight gym badges, then the Elite Four and the Champion. Gyms specialise in one type, which is why type matchups dominate play.
